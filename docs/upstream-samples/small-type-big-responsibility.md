Metadata is easy to overlook while designing the headline. Yet the date, category, and reading time tell a reader what kind of commitment they are about to make.

## Give metadata a predictable home

Align dates across a desktop list. Keep durations at the same edge. On a phone, move the title first and put the supporting details beneath it. The relationship survives even when the layout changes.

Monospace type can make this supporting layer feel distinct without adding another color. It also gives numbers a useful rhythm.

## Quiet is not invisible

Small text still needs readable contrast and enough space to avoid crowding. A faint gray that looks elegant on your monitor may disappear on a dim screen.

Check the real size at normal zoom. If a label needs to be read, it deserves to be legible.

## Let the headline lead

A label does not need to compete with the thing it describes. Use fewer words, consistent placement, and a calm weight. The reader should recognize the structure before noticing the styling.
